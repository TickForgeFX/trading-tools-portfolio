# Visual Risk and Position Size Calculator by TickForgeFX

Position sizing is the one risk rule every trader is told to follow, and most do it in their
head or skip it. This does it for you: drag your Entry, Stop and Target onto the chart and read
the exact **lot size** for the risk you choose, straight off the panel. No signals, no repaint,
no guesswork.

## What it shows

- **Lot size** for the percent of your account you choose to risk, respecting your broker's
  minimum, maximum and step.
- **Money at risk** in your account currency.
- **Stop distance** in points.
- **Reward-to-risk** and the potential profit at your target.
- **Direction**, long or short, read from your line positions.

## Why it helps

Getting size wrong is how a normal losing streak turns into a blown account. Set your risk once
(default 1% of balance), size off balance or equity, or type a fixed account size to plan a
different number. If the account is too small for your chosen risk at a given stop, it falls
back to the minimum lot and flags the real risk, so you are never quietly over-exposed.

## What it does not do

It does not place trades, fire signals, or predict direction. It is a calculator, and it does
the arithmetic correctly so you do not have to do it by hand. The decisions stay yours.
