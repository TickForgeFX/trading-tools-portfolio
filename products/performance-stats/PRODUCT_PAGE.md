# Trading Performance Statistics by TickForgeFX

A clean, free performance panel for MT5. It reads your closed trade history and totals up the
numbers most traders never actually sit down and calculate: net P/L, win rate, profit factor,
average win vs loss, payoff, expectancy, best and worst. It shows the truth, good or bad. It
never trades and fires no signals.

## What it shows

Attach it to any chart and a forge panel shows, live:

- **Net P/L** over the selected period, color-coded.
- **Trades**, and **win rate** with the win/loss count.
- **Profit factor** (gross profit divided by gross loss).
- **Average win** and **average loss**.
- **Payoff ratio** (average win to average loss).
- **Expectancy**, what an average trade is worth to you.
- **Best** and **worst** trade.

## Honest by design

- **Grouped per position.** A trade you closed in parts counts as one trade, not several, so
  the numbers match how you actually traded. (Partial closes are common, especially with a
  trade manager.)
- **It shows the truth.** A losing month reads as a losing month. This is a mirror, not a
  marketing tool.
- **Reads history only.** It cannot place, close, or influence a single trade.

## Filter it to what you want to see

- **Period:** all time, today, this week, this month, last 7 days, or last 30 days.
- **This chart's symbol only,** or every symbol.
- **By magic number,** to isolate one strategy or EA, or leave it at 0 for everything.

## Why it helps

Most traders never total their own numbers, so they never know whether they have an edge or a
leak. Win rate alone lies (a 40% win rate with a big payoff can be excellent). Seeing win rate,
profit factor, payoff and expectancy together, updated as you trade, turns a vague feeling into
a number you can act on. It also makes journaling faster, the totals are already done.

## What it does not do

It does not trade, predict, or fire signals. It measures what already happened. The decisions
stay yours.

## Recommended use

Attach it once on any chart. Set the period and filters to what you want to review. Empty
account? It says so cleanly and fills in as you close trades.
