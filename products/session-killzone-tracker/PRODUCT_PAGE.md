# Session and Killzone Tracker by TickForgeFX

Know which trading session is live, how long is left, and when the next one opens, with every
session's high and low drawn on the chart. This ICT Killzone and Session Tracker puts the
trading day on your chart, accurate on any broker because it runs on GMT. No signals, no
repaint.

## What it shows

- **Live session panel** with a GMT clock and a row for Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York,
  each showing active with the time left, or how long until it opens. The active session is
  highlighted in its colour.
- **Session range boxes** drawn from each session's high to its low across its hours, labelled
  and colour-coded, for the last few days. The current session's box grows in real time.

## Set to your killzones

The session times are plain GMT inputs. Leave them on the defaults for the standard sessions,
or set your exact killzone windows, for example a London killzone of 07:00 to 10:00 GMT. The
labels are editable too, so the panel and the boxes read the way you trade.

## Automatic daylight saving

You never re-time it twice a year. Each session carries a daylight-saving rule (Europe, US,
Australia, or none for Tokyo) and shifts by an hour through its local summer, so the panel and
boxes stay correct year-round. On by default, and optional if you would rather drive the times
manually.

## What it does not do

It does not place trades, fire signals, or predict direction. It tracks sessions and draws
their ranges. The decisions stay yours.

## Recommended use

Works on any symbol, and is best on intraday timeframes (M1 to H1) where the session ranges
matter. Free, and built to the TickForgeFX standard: clean, honest, no hype. If it earns a spot
on your charts, an honest review helps more than you know.
