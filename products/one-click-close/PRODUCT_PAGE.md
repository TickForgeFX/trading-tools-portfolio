# One-Click Close and Breakeven Panel

**Close or protect every open trade with one click.**

A fast exit cockpit for MetaTrader 5. When you need to act right now you can close everything, take only your winners, cut only your losers, flatten one direction, move every trade to break-even, or clear your pending orders, each from a single button. It opens no trades and fires no signals. It acts only on the positions you already hold, and only when you click.

## What it does
- **Close All** open positions in one click
- **Close Profits**, only the trades currently in profit
- **Close Losses**, only the trades currently in the red
- **Close Longs / Close Shorts**, flatten one direction
- **Breakeven All**, move the stop of every qualifying trade to its entry so it can no longer turn into a loss
- **Delete Pendings**, cancel all your pending orders

## Built to be safe and clear
- **Confirm step**: destructive buttons arm on the first click and act only on a second click, so a stray click never wipes your book
- **Color-coded feedback** after every click tells you exactly what happened, or why nothing did
- **Break-even** acts only on trades safely in profit, and never loosens a stop you already have
- Works **account-wide or symbol-only**, with an optional magic-number filter
- Works on both **netting and hedging** accounts

## The panel
A clean, draggable, lockable forge panel that always stays fully on your chart, showing your in-scope position count, pending orders, and live floating profit or loss at a glance.

## Honest by design
No signals. No auto-entries. No predictions. It closes and protects the trades you already hold, nothing more. Free on the MQL5 Market.
